# Proposal — Online Retail EDA

## Working title

**Online Retail EDA: sales performance, customer behaviour and cancellations**

## Business context

An online retailer wants to understand its sales performance and cancellations before making commercial decisions. The analysis should turn transaction records into a small set of reliable, understandable insights.

## Main question

What patterns in sales, customers and cancellations should the retailer understand to improve its commercial decisions?

## Objectives

- Assess the quality and structure of the transaction data.
- Quantify sales, revenue and cancellations over time.
- Identify products and countries with the greatest commercial contribution.
- Compare customer-level results without inventing missing customer identifiers.
- Produce recommendations supported by clear visual evidence.

## Non-goals

- Building a production recommendation system.
- Predicting future sales or cancellations.
- Imputing customer identifiers.
- Proving causal relationships.
- Building a dashboard or machine-learning model within the MVP.

## Success criteria

- The notebook runs from top to bottom without errors.
- Cleaning decisions are justified and reproducible.
- At least three self-defined business questions are answered in writing.
- At least five useful, labelled visualisations support the conclusions.
- The README explains the source, setup and execution.
- The executive summary is understandable without reading the code.
